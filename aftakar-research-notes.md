# Aftakar research notes

The official FIFA Legends page states that FIFA Legends recognises players and coaches who achieved something significant in football, considering domestic and international careers. Source: https://inside.fifa.com/organisation/fifa-legends

The official UEFA Champions League all-time matches-played page lists Cristiano Ronaldo first with 187 appearances, Iker Casillas second with 181, Gerd Müller third with 165, Manuel Neuer and Lionel Messi tied on 163, Xavi Hernández on 157, Luka Modrić and Toni Kroos tied on 153, and Karim Benzema on 152. Source: https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/history/rankings/players/matches_played/

These sources support using achievement-based, competition-based, and record-based clues alongside player position and rating. The current implementation keeps a curated metadata subset for nationality, clubs, achievements, and aliases, while generic catalogue questions remain clearly categorized as fallback questions.
